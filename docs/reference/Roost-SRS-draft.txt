1. Introduction
1.1 Purpose

This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) document defines the functional and non-functional requirements of Roost, a vacation rental and host management platform. The application aims to provide a convenient and reliable platform where guests can discover and book rental properties and hosts can manage property listings, availability, pricing, reservations, and guest communication. This document outlines the system's requirements, architecture, technology stack, and development plan to guide the design, implementation, and testing of Roost. 

1.2 Scope
The scope of this project includes the design, development, testing, and deployment of Roost, a vacation rental and host management platform, catering to:
Guests: Search and filter properties, save favorites to wishlists, verify their identity, book stays, message hosts, and leave reviews.

Hosts: Create listings, manage availability, pricing, and cancellation policies, offer promotional discounts, handle booking requests, coordinate deposits, cleaning, digital check-in, and stay reminders, and track earnings.

Co-Hosts and Property Managers: Help hosts manage listings, reservations, guest messages, and check-ins within the permissions the owner sets.

Administrators: Maintain the amenity catalog, monitor bookings, revenue, and platform activity, and resolve disputes.

This SRS covers the functional and non-functional requirements of Roost, including its architecture, technology stack, constraints, assumptions, and development plan. Physical property maintenance, transportation services and the direct operation of third-party payment systems and smart-lock hardware are outside the scope of the application. 






2. Overall Description
2.1 Product Perspective
Roost is a standalone web application that works as a marketplace between property hosts and guests, similar to Airbnb and Booking.com. It is not a module of an existing system and does not replace a hotel booking system. It gives independent hosts the tools that large platforms provide, such as listing management, calendar control, deposits, and guest messaging, in one place. It connects to outside services for payments, smart-lock access codes, and SMS or email notifications.
